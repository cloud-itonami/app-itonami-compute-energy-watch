# 許諾申請のドラフト（未送信）

**これは下書きであり、送信していない。** 送信は外部への連絡なので、owner の承認を得てから、owner が
送る（または送ってよいと指示する）。差出人名・所属・連絡先は owner が入れる。bot は自分では送らない。

共通の趣旨: itonami の分析 bot が、世界の電力・CPU・network・storage・GPU の需給を、公開出典から出典付きで
要約する。出典名・取得日・単位・対象期間を必ず付ける。投資助言には使わない。

## ITU（宛先: jur@itu.int）— 統計の再利用

件名: Permission request: reuse of ITU statistics with attribution

> We operate an analysis agent that summarises worldwide infrastructure indicators, including
> international bandwidth and Internet traffic, from public sources. We would like permission to
> retrieve selected ITU statistics (indicator names and years to be agreed) and to quote values with
> clear attribution (ITU as source, retrieval date, unit, period). We will not alter the figures, and
> we will not use the ITU name or logo beyond attribution. Please let us know the conditions, and
> whether a written licence is required.

## SEMI（宛先: mktstats@semi.org）— 外部公開の許諾

件名: Permission request: publishing derived figures from SEMI market data

> We would like to cite headline figures from SEMI's publicly released market statistics in a
> periodic digest, with attribution to SEMI and the publication date. We will not identify individual
> companies or fabs. Which published figures may be cited externally, and under what conditions?

## WSTS（宛先: サイトの連絡先を確認してから）— 出版物の引用

> We would like to cite headline figures from WSTS's publicly available statistics (for example the
> free Historical Billings Report) in a periodic digest with attribution. Please advise whether
> citing figures with attribution is permitted, and the conditions.

## Cloudflare Radar（宛先: Radar about ページの連絡先）— 商用の例外

> Cloudflare Radar API data is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0. Our digest may be offered as part of a
> commercial service. We would like to ask whether an exception, or a commercial licence, is
> available for citing aggregate Radar figures with attribution. If our use is non-commercial, please
> confirm.

注: Radar の digest が商用に当たるかは owner が先に判断する。当たらない場合は申請不要。
